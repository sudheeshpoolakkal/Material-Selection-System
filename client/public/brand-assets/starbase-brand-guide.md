# Starbase

Better foundations. Better decisions.

Starbase is an engineering material selection workspace for exploring source-linked material records, screening requirements, comparing alternatives, and saving the reasoning behind a decision.

## Name and signature

Write Starbase as one word with a capital S. When context is needed, use “Starbase — Material selection”. The four-point symbol represents direction and a shared foundation.

- starbase-logo-dark.svg: orange symbol and Carbon wordmark, for light surfaces.
- starbase-logo-light.svg: orange symbol and Chalk wordmark, for dark surfaces.
- starbase-mark.svg: standalone orange symbol, when the name is already clear.

SVG wordmarks use outlined shapes and do not require a font installation. All assets have transparent backgrounds.

Leave at least half the symbol width clear around the visible artwork. Keep the full signature at least 120 px wide and the standalone symbol at least 24 px wide. Preserve its proportions and orientation. Avoid effects and busy backgrounds.

## Color

Carbon #202322 — wordmarks, headings, and dark surfaces.
Signal #DF5630 — the symbol and moments of action.
Chalk #F7F8F6 — quiet, open backgrounds.
Alloy #DCE0DC — dividers and supporting surfaces.

Use Carbon for body text on light surfaces. Signal is an accent; use Carbon text on Signal for small labels. For white text buttons, use the darker action color #B84221 for readable contrast.

## Type

Inter, with 400, 500, 600, and 700 weights. The website serves Inter locally under the SIL Open Font License, included at /fonts/Inter-LICENSE.txt. The typeface was designed by Rasmus Andersson. Logo paths are derived from Inter Bold.

## Voice

Use direct, concrete language. Explain the decision before the detail. State what evidence supports, keep unknown values visible, and preserve room for engineering judgment. Avoid unverified claims, exaggerated performance promises, and invented endorsements.
